How a case works

  1. Register and verify yourself

    Create your account with a government ID, a selfie, and codes sent to your phone and email.

  2. Add your device details

    Give the make, model, IMEI or serial number, proof of purchase, and where and when you lost it.

  3. Sign the agreement online

    Read the terms and e-sign them. This authorises us to work on your case.

  4. Set up the refundable hold

    A card hold is placed. It is charged only if the device is recovered.

  5. Start the search

    You sign in on Google's or Apple's own page to mark the device lost. We never see or store your password.

  6. Follow your case

    See the last known location and every action taken, in one place.

  7. Get it back safely

    We guide the handover to a public place, a verified drop-off point, or with police help.

  8. Confirm and pay

    Confirm you have the device. The fee is charged. If it wasn't recovered, the hold is released.

Questions

Do you need my account password?

No. You sign in on Google's or Apple's own page. We never see or store your password.

Why do you verify my identity?

It keeps fraudsters from claiming devices that aren't theirs, and it protects you.

What happens to my documents?

We keep only what verification needs, store it encrypted, and delete or archive it when your case closes.

What if the device can't be found?

You owe nothing. Your card hold is released.
